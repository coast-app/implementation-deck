04

Stage 1 · Week 1

The Implementation Planning Meeting

One hour with your specialist — the IPM. We confirm what you need Coast to do, walk your account, agree exactly which data we need, and set the dates that everything else hangs off.

What we cover

  • Your objectives and how your team works today
  • The workflows you need Coast to support
  • Who needs access, and at what permission level
  • A walkthrough of your account
  • Exactly which data your workflows need, and how to send it

Four things we decide live

  1. Your project owner — the one person we work with throughout
  2. The data you're sending — confirmed against your workflows, not a generic list
  3. Your data due date — set on the call, not emailed afterward
  4. Your target launch date — the date we both work back from
We fill in the first few rows together.

Before the call ends, we open the data template and complete the first entries with you, so the format is never a guess.

    06

    Being straight with you

    What happens if data is late

    No surprises. If your data slips, your launch date slips with it — here is exactly what that looks like.

    Due date + 1 day
    A friendly check-in from your specialist
    + 3 days
    A second follow-up, by email and phone
    + 5 days
    Your account executive is looped in to help unblock
    + 10 days
    Implementation is paused until data arrives
    Pausing is not cancelling.

    A paused implementation can restart whenever you are ready. We pause so your specialist's time goes to customers who are actively moving, and so we are honest with you about where the project stands rather than quietly letting it drift.

    Configuration timelines shift day for day with late data. If your data arrives a week late, your launch moves by a week.

    07

    Stage 3

    Building your account

    We import your data and configure Coast around the way your team actually works. You review it before anyone gets trained on it.

    Standard setup

    Our out-of-the-box maintenance configuration:

    • Work orders and requests
    • Assets, locations, and parts
    • Preventive maintenance schedules
    • Checklists and procedures
    • Users, roles, and permissions

    Custom configuration

    Where most of our customers land. Adds:

    • Workflows shaped to your process
    • Automations and notifications
    • Integrations with your other systems
    • Custom fields, views, and dashboards

    Scope is confirmed at your planning meeting.

    You review before we train.

    We walk you through the configured account and change what needs changing. It is much cheaper to fix a workflow before twenty people have learned it.

    08

    Stage 4

    Training your team

    Two sessions, built around what each group actually needs to do. Live, recorded, and yours to keep.

    Session 1 — Admins

    Your project owner and anyone managing Coast day to day.

    Afterwards they can: create and assign work orders, manage assets and parts, adjust PM schedules, add users, and pull reports.

    Session 2 — Your team

    Technicians and anyone completing or requesting work.

    Afterwards they can: pick up assigned work, complete checklists, log parts used, submit requests, and update work from their phone.

    We book your Launch Review during this session.

    Before training ends, we put a date in the calendar for a week's time. Questions always come up once people start using Coast for real, and that call is where we handle them.

    09

    Stage 5 · Final week

    Go-Live Week

    Training is not the finish line. Your team uses Coast on real work for a week, and then we sit down together and fix whatever came up.

    During the week

    Your team runs live work through Coast. Real work orders, real assets, real people. Everything else stays as-is until you are confident.

    Keep a running list

    Anything that feels wrong, missing, or confusing — write it down as it happens. Your specialist will set up somewhere to collect these.

    The Launch Review

    We go through your list together, make the tweaks, confirm everything is set up correctly, and answer whatever is still open.

    Your implementation is complete after the Launch Review.

    Not after training. We close out once you have used Coast on real work and told us it is doing what you need. From there you move to ongoing support and regular check-ins.

    10

    If this applies to you

    Phased implementations

    Some operations are too complex to bring live all at once. When that is the case, we split the work into phases and get you using Coast sooner rather than waiting for everything to be perfect.

    Phase 1

    Gets you live and working on your core workflows — usually work orders, assets, and preventive maintenance. This is what the timeline in this guide covers.

    Later phases

    Additional sites, departments, integrations, or workflows, each scoped and scheduled separately once Phase 1 is live and your team is comfortable.

    The timeline in this guide applies to Phase 1.

    If your implementation is phased, the 5-week schedule covers getting Phase 1 live. Your specialist will give you a separate plan covering what lands in each phase and when. Phasing is confirmed at your planning meeting.
